FAQ

Your role and what your plan covers.

Do I have to appear on camera?

You, your founder or a team member can present. Alejandro may also appear when the agreed concept benefits from it, with no additional talent fee. Special shoots, travel and external costs are quoted separately.

Do you film the videos?

The base service uses footage recorded by you or your team with a phone and our guidance. In-person or professional productions can be quoted separately.

Is publication to Alejandro’s audience included?

No. These plans cover content for your brand’s agreed accounts. Distribution through Alejandro’s personal channels is a separately scoped collaboration.

Does this include paid ads or community management?

Paid campaign management, media spend and daily replies to comments or messages are separate. You can combine this service with campaign management.

Can you guarantee views or sales?

No. We improve content quality, clarity and consistency. Reach and commercial results also depend on your audience, offer and sales process.
