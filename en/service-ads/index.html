FAQ

Before we launch.

Does the fee include advertising spend?

No. Your monthly fee covers campaign strategy, creative production and management. You pay media spend directly to the selected platform.

Will my campaigns run on all three platforms?

Only when your audience, budget and sales process support it. We recommend the channel mix that fits your business.

Who appears in the video ads?

You, a member of your team, or Alejandro when the concept calls for it and it is agreed in advance. His appearance carries no extra talent fee. Travel and special production costs are quoted separately.

Do I need a website?

Some campaigns can use native forms or WhatsApp. Others benefit from a landing page. Website development, CRM services and external tools are quoted separately.

Are sales or a specific return guaranteed?

No. Results also depend on your offer, market, budget and sales follow-up. We manage and optimize using actual campaign data.
